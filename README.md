# SplitSnap 💸

### AI-Powered Bill Splitting from UPI/SMS Payment Alerts

SplitSnap is an AI-powered bill-splitting application designed for friends, roommates, and groups who frequently share expenses.

Instead of manually entering every shared expense, SplitSnap analyzes simulated payment information, identifies potential shared expenses, helps users split bills, tracks balances, and simplifies settlement.

> 🚀 Built as a hackathon prototype for the FinTech & Commerce track.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

When people share expenses, it can be difficult to remember:

- Who paid?
- Who owes money?
- How much does each person owe?
- Who should pay whom?

Traditional bill-splitting methods often require manual entry and calculations.

SplitSnap aims to make this process simpler and faster.

---

## 💡 Solution

SplitSnap follows this workflow:

```text
UPI / SMS Payment Alert
          ↓
      AI Analysis
          ↓
   Shared Expense Detection
          ↓
   Select Participants
          ↓
  Equal / Custom Split
          ↓
   Balance Calculation
          ↓
    Smart Settlement
          ↓
  Simulated UPI Payment
